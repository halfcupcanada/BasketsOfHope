A community event, 15 years running.

Rohit's Baskets of Hope is an annual community giving event that collects monetary and in-kind donations to fill comfort baskets for women and families escaping domestic violence — in partnership with WIN House Edmonton. Guests, businesses, and volunteers come together each November to make it happen.

Learn more about the event →

More than practical — a reminder that someone cares.

Baskets offer dignity and comfort during a difficult transition, not just the basics. Cozy socks, a journal, body care, a small blanket — small things that carry a real message: you are seen, valued, and not alone.
