About Rohit's Baskets of Hope

Rohit's Baskets of Hope is a community giving event started in 2010 by Rohit Group's co-founder Krishna Gupta and her dear friend, Dianne Young. What began as a gathering of friends has grown into an annual tradition that brings businesses, volunteers, and community members together in support of WIN House.

The idea is simple: when someone is rebuilding after violence, a small act of care can carry a powerful message. Each basket is filled with comfort items that help recipients feel seen, supported, and worthy of moments of peace.

Why baskets?

WIN House and other community supports help meet urgent needs. Baskets of Hope adds something personal: items that encourage self-care, comfort, and mindful "me-time" moments during a difficult transition.

A basket might include cozy socks, a journal, body care, a small blanket, or other thoughtful items. The contents are simple, but the message is meaningful: you are not alone, and your community is standing with you.

Remembering Dianne Young

Baskets of Hope began in the living room of Krishna's dear friend, Dianne Young, where her giving spirit brought 12 friends together around a simple idea: to offer comfort, dignity, and hope to women in need.

What started as a small act of care has grown into a community tradition that continues to remind every recipient that they are seen, valued, and not alone.

We will dearly miss Dianne, and know her warmth, kindness, and quiet generosity live on in every basket packed, every gift given, and every person who carries her spirit of compassion forward.

Who we support

In Edmonton, Baskets of Hope supports WIN House, which provides refuge, support, and advocacy for women, non-binary individuals, and children fleeing domestic violence.

Through this partnership, baskets and donations are directed with care and respect for the privacy and dignity of the people receiving them.
